SafeRide is designed to detect potential vehicle crashes, obtain GPS location and help notify emergency contacts when the driver cannot.

The problem

What if you couldn’t call for help?

Most of us assume we could reach out if something went wrong. Sometimes that isn’t possible, and the people waiting at home don’t know.

Unable to call

After an accident, a driver may be injured, shaken or unconscious, and unable to contact their family.

Location is hard to share

Sending a precise location by hand may not be possible in the moments that matter most.

Older vehicles are left out

Many older and budget vehicles have no built-in accident notification system.

How SafeRide works

Four steps, designed to run on their own.

1

Detect

2

Verify

3

Locate

4

Alert

The system is designed to notify configured emergency contacts.

Meet the technology

Compact hardware you can retrofit.

SafeRide is a retrofit-friendly hardware concept: a small unit that works with the vehicle you already own.

  • ESP32Microcontroller
  • MPU6050Accelerometer + gyroscope
  • Vibration sensorImpact input
  • GPS moduleLocation
  • GSM moduleCommunication
  • OLED displayStatus + countdown
  • Buzzer + LEDsLocal alerts
  • Cancel buttonDismiss false alarms

Sense. The MPU6050 and vibration sensor feed motion data to the ESP32.

Decide. When a potential impact is flagged, the buzzer, LEDs and OLED start a cancellation countdown.

Communicate. If it isn’t cancelled, the GPS module gets a position and the GSM module is used to notify configured contacts.

Review. The dashboard presents vehicle status and incident history.
